Our story

She Blew My Money. Now She Doesn't Have To.

A weave and wig subscription, built by a dad whose wallet had seen enough.

Every few weeks, the ask came.

Money for a wig. Money for weave. Money for products I couldn't pronounce. And every time, I paid it.

I'm a father. I want my girls to walk out the door feeling their best, because when they put their best selves forward, everything else follows. So I handed over my hard-earned money without complaint. (Okay, with a little complaint.)

I'm also a poor father. Emphasis on poor.

One day I looked at the receipts and realized that "she blew my money" wasn't a joke anymore. It was a budget line. And I knew I couldn't be the only dad, mom, or grandparent quietly funding a hair habit one rush order at a time.

So I built the thing I wished I'd had: a subscription that gives your girl fresh, quality hair on a schedule, at a price that doesn't hurt.
