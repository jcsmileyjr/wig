How it works

Three simple steps, from quiz to your front door.

  1. 1

    Take the quiz and pick a plan

    Choose wigs or weaves, synthetic or human hair, and your cap size. Tell us your texture, length, and anything you never want sent.

  2. 2

    Your box arrives

    Wigs ship ready to wear. Weave boxes ship new and unworn: monthly for synthetic, every 90 days for human hair.

  3. 3

    Keep it or swap it

    Synthetic wigs and weaves are yours to keep. Luxury human hair wigs go back in the prepaid package, and your next one is on its way.

Quick answers

Has someone else worn my wig or weave?

Synthetic wigs and weave boxes ship new and unworn. Luxury human hair wigs are swapped and returned, so each one is cleaned, sanitized, inspected, and sealed before it ships to you.

Do I keep my wig or weave?

Yes for the Everyday Synthetic Box, the Synthetic Weave Box, and the Complete Install Box. The Luxury Wig and Luxury Swap plans are swaps: you send the wig back in the prepaid package.

Which plan is right for me?

Start with the Everyday Synthetic Box for an affordable wig to keep each month. Choose a luxury plan for human hair wigs you swap out, or a weave box if you're planning an install. The style quiz can point you to the right one.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can pause, skip, or cancel from your account. Add any notice period here if one applies.

Is it human hair or synthetic?

Both. The Everyday Synthetic Box and the Synthetic Weave Box use synthetic hair. The Luxury Wig, Luxury Swap, and Complete Install Box use human hair, and luxury wigs are pre-cut with glueless closures.
