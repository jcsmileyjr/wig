Styles we carry

A look for every day, every occasion, and every comfort level.

Wig styles

Ready to wear, no styling needed.

Glueless “Wear-and-Go” HD Lace

Pre-cut lace, pre-plucked hairlines, and elastic bands that need no adhesive or gel. Usually worn in a middle-part bone straight or loose body wave.

Synthetic and human hair

Blunt Cut / Asymmetrical Bob

Sharp 10 to 14 inch bobs, often built on 4x4 or 5x5 closures. Bobs hold their shape better on a wig cap than on a natural hair install.

Synthetic and human hair

Lace Frontal Deep Wave (13x4 / 13x6)

Lace running from ear to ear for deep side parts, half-up and half-down styles, and braided crown details with defined curls.

Synthetic only

Headband & V-Part / U-Part

Zero-lace, beginner-friendly options. Headband wigs slip on with no hairline showing. V-parts leave a small sliver of your own hair out for a seamless blend without glue.

Synthetic and human hair

Bangs & Fringe Units

Curtain bangs, blunt fringes, or wispy bangs that hide the front edge, so the cap is invisible even without lace.

Synthetic and human hair

Weave styles

Boxes contain hair only. Bring yours to your stylist for installation.

Traditional Leave-Out

Your stylist braids down your natural hair and leaves out a small horseshoe section at the crown. Heat styled to match the bundles, it gives the most undetectable scalp look.

Either weave box

Closure Sew-In (4x4, 5x5, 6x6)

Fully protects the natural hair underneath with no leave-out. The lace closure sits at the center or side for a natural-looking part without heat damage.

Complete Install Box

2-Part / 3-Part Sew-In (Flip-Over)

Track placement lets you flip your hair side to side, wear high ponytails, or switch from a center to a side part without redoing the install.

Either weave box

Raw Curly / Deep Wave Vacation Install

Three to four full bundles of textured curls for low-maintenance, wet-and-go wear that holds volume in high humidity.

Complete Install Box

Genius Wefts & Micro-Link (Braidless)

Ultra-thin tracks attached to micro-beaded rows of your own hair instead of cornrows. They lay completely flat for sleek top-knots and easy ponytails.

Complete Install Box

How to measure your head

  1. 1
    Use a soft tape measure and pull your hair back flat.
  2. 2
    Start at the center of your forehead, at the hairline.
  3. 3
    Wrap the tape behind your ears and around the nape of your neck, back to where you started.
  4. 4
    Match the number to the chart. Between sizes? Contact us and we'll help you choose.
